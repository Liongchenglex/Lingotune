SECTION 1 — Hangul & Reading (Q1–Q4)
Q1 — Hangul Decomposition

Type: Multiple choice
Question:
Which correctly breaks down the syllable 밤?

A. ㅂ + ㅏ + ㅁ
B. ㅂ + ㅏ
C. ㅂ + ㅁ
D. ㅏ + ㅁ

✅ Answer: A

Hangul decoding

받침 understanding

Type: Read & answer
